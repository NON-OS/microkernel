6. BRAND, TYPE AND TOOLS
------------------------

  Wordmark: userland/assets/wallet_logos/nonos-logo-teal.svg (its letters,
    in the file's own colours), source in that folder's SOURCES.md.
  Palette: Ink #0A0B0D, Cyan #66FFFF, Teal #007C85, Card, Rule and the
    text greys: userland/capsule_install/brand/src/palette.rs and
    userland/toolkit/src/theme/schemes/nonos.rs.
  Fonts: Geist SemiBold, Medium (userland/assets/fonts/Geist-*.ttf,
    SIL Open Font License 1.1, Geist-OFL.txt); JetBrains Mono Regular
    (userland/assets/fonts/JetBrainsMono-Regular.ttf, SIL Open Font
    License 1.1, JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt).
  Made with Python, Pillow and NumPy (the set, the camera and the type)
    and FFmpeg with libx264 (the encode).

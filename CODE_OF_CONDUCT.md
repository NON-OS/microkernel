# Code of Conduct

NONOS is a systems project. The work is exacting and the review is direct, and
that is not the same thing as being unpleasant to each other.

## What is expected

Argue about the code. Bring evidence: a serial log, a failing proof, a diff. If
you think something is wrong, say so plainly and say why. A blunt technical
disagreement is welcome and is most of what happens here.

Assume the person on the other side is competent and acting in good faith until
they show you otherwise. People arrive with different backgrounds, hardware and
amounts of free time, and none of that is a measure of what they are worth to
the project.

If you are corrected, take the correction. If you are wrong about something you
argued hard for, saying so is not a loss.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, or making someone's identity the subject.
Sustained hostility toward a person rather than their work. Publishing private
information. Deliberately wasting people's time.

None of that becomes acceptable because the technical point underneath it was
correct.

## Scope

This applies in the repositories, the issue tracker, pull requests, and the
project chat. It applies to maintainers at least as much as to anyone else.

## Reporting

Mail **ekisanon@proton.me**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
private. Say what happened and where; a link is usually enough.

Depending on what is found, the response ranges from a private word to removal
from the project's spaces. Reports about a maintainer are handled by the other
maintainers, and that maintainer is not part of deciding the outcome.

## Security reports are a different channel

Anything exploitable goes through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/NON-OS/nonos-kernel/security/advisories/new),
not here. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

# MediaTek MT7921 and MT7922

The mt7921e driver [capsule](../../overview/glossary-boot-root-record.md#capsule) runs the MediaTek MT7921 (MT7921E, AMD RZ608), MT7922 (AMD RZ616, in the ThinkPad T14 Gen 4 AMD among many) and MT7920 PCIe Wi-Fi cards: it brings the chip up, scans 2.4 and 5 GHz, joins WPA2 and WPA3 networks through the shared station and carries traffic for `net.core`.

## State

Host-proven only. No boot on a card has run it. Every statement below is read from the code and from `userland/mt7921_proofs`, which mounts the driver's own source over a modelled chip and MCU and the shared proofs' access points: bring-up to `Ready`, a scan that hears the network, a WPA2 join that keys the link, a WPA3 join (SAE by hash-to-element, PMF required, on 5 GHz channel 44) and a join of a WPA3 transition-mode network (PSK and SAE offered; SAE chosen), and data sealed out and opened in. The model's air hears a frame only at a rate the access point decodes (no CCK on 5 GHz, none at an access point with 802.11b off), and its firmware can take the card back (ASPM, runtime PM) and forget the WFDMA's rings. The [real-hardware checklist](../../hardware/real-hardware-checklist.md#mediatek-mt7921-and-mt7922) is its first test.

## Identity

The six ids of Linux v6.12's `mt7921_pci_device_table` (`drivers/net/wireless/mediatek/mt76/mt7921/pci.c`): 14c3:7961, 14c3:7922, 0b48:7922, 14c3:0608, 14c3:0616, 14c3:7920 (`userland/capsule_driver_mt7921/src/ids.rs`, `PCI_IDS`). The kernel's inventory (`src/hardware/inventory/mediatek_ids.rs`), the broker's scope row (`src/hardware/broker/scope_rows.rs`, `MT7921`) and Settings (`userland/capsule_settings/src/wifi/driver_ids.rs`) name the same six; `kernel_proofs` holds the inventory and the row to the driver's list and `scripts/check_driver_scope.py` the row. The chip is read back (`MT_HW_CHIPID`, `MT_HW_BOUND` bit 7 for the MT7920) and decides the firmware.

The kernel starts the capsule only when the boot scan found one of the six (`src/userspace/init/spawn_plan/drivers_wifi/mediatek.rs`). Its service is `driver.mt7921e0` on port 4266, reply port 4267 (`userland/capsule_driver_mt7921/Capsule.mk`).

## Authority

The mask 0xB8038: IPC, Memory, Crypto, Driver, DeviceEnum, Mmio and Dma, with Debug (0x100) optional, as the RTL8821CE's. No Irq: the rings are polled and the chip's interrupts stay masked. Crypto draws the station address (locally administered, never the card's own) and the handshake nonces. DeviceEnum also admits `MkInstallSource`, which is how the firmware package is read. No FileSystem, no Network.

A firmware failure is retried three times (Linux retries ten), the card reset between attempts; after the third the WFDMA is stopped and Bus Master turned off, and the driver serves its stage.

### Where it departs from Linux

- Scanning is the shared station's: it tunes to a channel by a channel request (ROC) and listens, rather than sending `MCU_CE_CMD_START_HW_SCAN`; the station's own sweep, dwell and world-safe channel rules then apply unchanged, as on the other two drivers. A join holds its channel by a `JOIN` request, renewed before it lapses, until the BSS context takes the channel at association.
- Keys are never installed in the chip: CCMP runs in software in the shared station, so every frame crosses the ring in the clear and the chip is told nothing about keys.
- Every frame goes out at a fixed rate. As Linux (`mt76_connac2_mac_tx_rate_val`: the lowest of the BSS's basic rates, which mac80211 takes from the beacon before authenticating), a frame to the access point goes at the lowest basic rate it advertised (read from its beacons and probe responses, then from the association response), anything else at the band's lowest; unlike Linux, which lets the firmware's rate control pick, data to the access point goes at its fastest basic rate, and its EAPOL frames at the lowest.
- A channel grant is taken from the data ring as well as the event rings, and is the request's when it names its token or its channel (Linux reads neither).
- The transmit power limits are sent as Linux sends them with no device tree, SAR or ACPI table (20 dBm on each world-domain channel, 127 elsewhere). No CLC tables are sent: Linux's `disable_clc` path, the firmware's defaults.
- No interrupts, no power save, no 6 GHz, no header translation, no A-MSDU de-aggregation.

## The firmware package

The firmware is not in the tree, in the capsule or in the kernel. Linux-firmware's six files at commit bf992df7a95b5e19bb8f8546eb4d7c53ab2598d0 (the MT7961, MT7961_1a and MT7922 patch and RAM code; WHENCE: Redistributable, `LICENCE.mediatek`) are pinned once, by name, length and sha256, in `userland/capsule_driver_mt7921/src/fw/pins.rs`.

- The image build places them, with `LICENCE.mediatek`, at `EFI/nonos/firmware/mediatek/` on the boot medium: `scripts/firmware_package.py`, called by `make nonos-mk-esp` from a linux-firmware tree (`NONOS_FIRMWARE_SOURCE`) or fetched once into `target/`; the nix artifact tree carries them under `firmware/mediatek/` (`tools/nix/firmware.nix`) and the seal's ESP (`tools/nonos_seal/media.py`, which a sealed image and `tools/nonos-dev-image` are packed from) places them from there. A medium packed without them still boots; the driver then reports stage 10 and the panel reads "The firmware package is missing from the boot medium".
- The loader asks whether each file is there, then reads it into loader memory and records it as a handoff module, kinds 0x20 to 0x25 (`nonos-bootloader/src/entry/firmware_package.rs`); an absent file is passed over without a line. It checks nothing about them.
- The kernel serves those kinds through `MkInstallSource` to a holder of DeviceEnum (`src/syscall/microkernel/install_source_kinds.rs`, `is_firmware`).
- The driver reads its chip's two files and refuses either unless its length and sha256 are the pinned ones (stage 11).
- `scripts/check_mediatek_firmware.py`, run by the static checks, holds the loader's paths, the kernel's kinds and the nix list to the pins, runs the seal's ESP on stand-ins to see each file land where the loader reads it, and fails on a pinned file, an `include_bytes!` of one, or (given the files) any run of their bytes in the tree or in a built capsule or kernel.
- An installed disk does not carry the package: the installer writes the ESP's own files, not these. Wi-Fi on an installed system reports stage 10 until it does.

## Tests

`userland/mt7921_proofs`: 122 tests, the driver's own source over a modelled chip (register window, ownership and its loss, reset, a WFDMA left busy, rings, MCU command and event protocol, patch semaphore, download, channel grants on either ring, the air's rates), the shared WPA2 access point simulator and the RTL8852 proofs' WPA3 access point (pure and in transition mode). Synthetic firmware images with the real header layouts are built in the tests; one test reads the real pinned files from a directory outside the tree when `NONOS_MT7921_PACKAGE` names one, and does nothing otherwise. `verification/host_mutants/mt7921.json`: 70 mutants, each removing one rule from the driver's, the loader's or the seal's source, all caught.

## Not done

- A boot on a card. Until then nothing here is known to work on hardware.
- SAE by hunting-and-pecking (an access point without hash-to-element) is not modelled here.
- The chip's interrupts, power save, hardware scan, key offload, rate control, A-MPDU, CLC and SAR tables, 6 GHz, the MT7925.
- The package on an installed disk.
